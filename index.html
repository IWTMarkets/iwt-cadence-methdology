IWT Cadence: The Luxury Sales Nowcasting Model

IWT Cadence is a high-frequency forecast of luxury industry sales growth. It reads a revenue-weighted index of 17 listed houses at half-year frequency and updates every weekday.

The model separates what luxury houses sell from what exchange rates do to the sales they report. Demand is modelled at constant currency, anchored in global wealth and Chinese output. Translation is computed from each house's regional mix and daily European Central Bank reference rates. For the current half, a bridge from published quarterly sales supplies a nowcast, weighted against the model by its historical accuracy.
